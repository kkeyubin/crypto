# Product Vision

## Mission

Create a personal cryptocurrency research laboratory that turns trading ideas into versioned hypotheses, tests them against reproducible market data, operates them in live paper trading, and reports evidence without claiming guaranteed profitability.

## Owner Outcome

The owner can add a Binance USDⓈ-M symbol, acquire verified historical and live data, define a strategy through the unified Nison/Volman/Aronson Skill, inspect its complete research history, enable a frozen version in paper trading, and receive explainable Feishu alerts.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before activation.
2. Context and chronology before pattern names.
3. One frozen strategy version per result.
4. Historical and live modes share execution semantics.
5. Failed experiments and searched parameters remain visible.
6. AI is an auditable analyst, not an invisible order authority.
7. Missing or stale data fails closed for new positions.
8. Deferred ideas remain in the roadmap with prerequisites.

## MVP Scope

- Binance USDⓈ-M market data.
- Dynamic symbol watchlist.
- Historical backtest and replay.
- BB/RB deterministic strategy families.
- Long/short perpetual paper trading, default `1x` leverage.
- Aronson evidence reports.
- AI shadow assessments using the unified Skill.
- Hermes `crypto` profile to Feishu notifications.
- Single-user LAN Web console on `192.168.1.4`.

## Non-Goals

The MVP does not place real orders, store exchange credentials, expose a public multi-user service, consume news/social sentiment, or let AI modify orders. See [the roadmap](../roadmap.md) for preserved later stages.
