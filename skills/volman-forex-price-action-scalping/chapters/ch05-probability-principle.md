# Chapter 5 — The Probability Principle

## Core lesson

No price-action setup predicts an individual outcome. A trader acts only when contextual pressure makes one path more plausible, accepts losses as part of the distribution, and judges quality across a series.

A skipped setup that later wins does not make the skip wrong; a weak decision that wins does not become sound.

## Research use

Predefine the signal, benchmark, costs, sample, and success metric. Report uncertainty and full distributions. Do not use selected chart examples as statistical validation.
