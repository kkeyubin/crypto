# Chapter 1 — Trading Currencies

## Core lesson

The method was designed for the highly liquid EUR/USD pair, where small intraday movements, tight transaction costs, and continuous participation made short targets plausible. Market choice is part of the strategy rather than a neutral wrapper.

## Research use

Record venue liquidity, spread, fees, trading hours, leverage, and outage risk. A setup observed in one crypto venue is not automatically executable or portable elsewhere.
