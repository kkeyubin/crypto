# Chapter 3 — Scalping as a Business

## Core lesson

Scalping demands repetition, selectivity, stable execution, and emotional control. The professional stance is to observe most of the time and act only when the complete proposition is favorable. More trades do not imply more edge.

## Research use

Measure signal frequency, capacity, latency sensitivity, operational errors, and drawdown—not just average return. Keep a decision log that scores correct process independently of each trade's outcome.
