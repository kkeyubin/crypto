# Chapter 8 — First Break (FB)

## Structure

FB follows a fast, forceful, one-directional surge, preferably emerging from consolidation. It uses the first substantial pullback of that new move, often a relatively straight 40–60% retracement near the 20 EMA area. The first pullback bar taken out in the trend direction supplies the trigger, producing the characteristic fishhook continuation.

## Filters

All three elements matter: exceptional surge, meaningful pullback, and first-pullback status. The EMA may lag a strong move or be crossed by a fast retracement; it is guidance, not a gate.

## Failure mode

Most apparent first breaks should be skipped. A routine EMA touch or a late pullback in an aging trend is not FB.
