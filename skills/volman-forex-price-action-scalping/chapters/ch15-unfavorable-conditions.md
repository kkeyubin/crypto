# Chapter 15 — Unfavorable Conditions

## Core lesson

The author expects an observational stance roughly 90% of the time. A recognizable setup is vetoed when pressure is unclear, the target path is blocked, nearby clustering or defended support/resistance dominates, the structure is too wide, or a breakout lacks preparation.

Round-number defense and congested chart areas matter only when price behavior shows that traders are reacting there. They are not automatic lines.

## Decision discipline

A rejected trade may later win. Evaluate whether the information available at entry justified the veto, not whether hindsight shows the target was reachable.
