# Chapter 6 — Setups

## Core lesson

The seven setups are entry mechanisms within a broader reading of pressure. The same shape may be valid in one location and invalid in another. Shared requirements are directional evidence, a visible trigger, sufficient target space, and controlled invalidation.

## Seven families

DD, FB, and SB organize trend pullbacks. BB captures compact compression. RB addresses a prepared break of a mature range. IRB trades a block within a larger range. ARB enters after price has already moved outside the old range.
