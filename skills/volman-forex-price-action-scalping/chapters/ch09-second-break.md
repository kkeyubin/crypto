# Chapter 9 — Second Break (SB)

## Structure

SB is a sequence:

```text
trend -> pullback -> first with-trend break -> weak/no continuation
-> renewed countertrend pressure -> second with-trend break
```

The first break is normally observed rather than traded. When the pullback resumes but cannot destroy the larger trend, the second attack through a newly formed signal bar is the entry.

## Filters

Favor an orderly diagonal pullback, persistent trend pressure, and an unobstructed path. Define precisely what constitutes a break attempt and failed follow-through.

## Failure mode

SB does not mean two candles or a generic second touch. It counts two chronological attempts to resume the trend.
