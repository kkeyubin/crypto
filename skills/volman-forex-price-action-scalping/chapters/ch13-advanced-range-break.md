# Chapter 13 — Advanced Range Break (ARB)

## Structure

ARB handles a range that has already been crossed without offering a clean standard RB.

### Post-break cluster

Price remains around the broken barrier—above it for a long or below for a short—without proving the break false. It compresses into a new cluster whose trigger line lies outside the original range.

### Breakout pullback

Price breaks out, returns to the old barrier, and finds that prior resistance/support now caps the pullback. A signal bar is then broken back in the original breakout direction.

## Critical distinction

RB triggers at the original range boundary after pre-break tension. ARB triggers at a new, outside-range signal line formed by post-break pressure or a pullback.
