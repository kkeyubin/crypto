# Chapter 11 — Range Break (RB)

## Structure

RB trades the original barrier of a mature horizontal range. The ideal range has at least two approximately equal highs and two lows. In the original chart it might persist from about 15 minutes to several hours, often near an hour; the principle is visibility and accumulated participation, not the clock duration.

Before entry, small bars should press against one boundary while opposing traders fail to push price away. This pre-breakout tension prepares the break. The boundary itself is the signal line.

## Traps

- **False break**: price travels directly from the opposite side and pierces the edge without buildup, often exhausted.
- **Tease break**: a small premature poke before sufficient preparation.

00/50 zones often shaped the original EUR/USD ranges but require fresh measurement in other markets.
