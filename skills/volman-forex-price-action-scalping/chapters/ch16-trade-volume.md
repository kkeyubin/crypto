# Chapter 16 — Trade Volume

## Core lesson

The book illustrates sizing a 10-pip maximum loss at no more than about 2% of account equity and adjusting volume as equity changes. It also shows dramatic compounding arithmetic, but those examples are mathematical illustrations rather than promised outcomes.

Traders without stable technical and emotional execution should use very small size until monetary fluctuations become psychologically unimportant.

## Research use

Separate signal edge from sizing. Model leverage, liquidation, drawdown, correlated exposure, daily loss limits, funding, and changing fees. Stress-test risk below the author's illustrative ceiling.
