# Chapter 17 — Words of Caution

## Core lesson

The author presents a personal method, not definitive rules or scientific proof. Historical charts demonstrate interpretation, not guaranteed future behavior. Readers are told to study, test against historical and current charts, proceed lightly, and reduce volume while uncertain.

## Research use

Preserve this epistemic status. Convert judgment into testable definitions, quantify label agreement, include realistic costs and untouched data, and be willing to conclude that a setup is ambiguous or unsupported.
