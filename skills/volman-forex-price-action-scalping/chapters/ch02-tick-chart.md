# Chapter 2 — The Tick Chart

## Core lesson

The author's 70-tick chart completes one candle after 70 recorded transactions. It adapts activity density: active periods create bars quickly and quiet periods slowly. The number is practical, not universal; nearby settings such as 65 or 75 would look similar.

Only a 20 EMA is displayed, as an aid to reading trend and pullback—not as a trading signal by itself.

## Research use

Define exactly what the feed calls a trade and when a bar becomes complete. Compare fixed-count, time, volume, and dollar bars. Never infer an intrabar event sequence from final OHLC alone.
