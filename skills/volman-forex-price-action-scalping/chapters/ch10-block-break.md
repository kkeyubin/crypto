# Chapter 10 — Block Break (BB)

## Structure

Several bars compress into a narrow vertical block or minirange. Its top and bottom preferably receive multiple touches, making both barriers visible. Entry occurs only when price breaks the barrier aligned with existing pressure—the path of least resistance. Smaller compression at the chosen edge adds pre-breakout tension.

## Contexts

BB can organize a trend pullback, continuation after an explosive move with no classic pullback, movement within a range, or selected top/bottom situations. Context supplies direction; the block does not.

## Failure mode

Do not bracket both sides mechanically. A break of the unfavorable edge may simply mean no trade, not an automatic reversal.
