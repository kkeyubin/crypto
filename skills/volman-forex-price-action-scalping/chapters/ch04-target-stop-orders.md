# Chapter 4 — Target, Stop, and Orders

## Original template

- Fixed 10-pip profit target.
- Maximum initial protection of 10 pips.
- Structure usually permits a tighter 6–7 pip technical stop.
- A stop can move only toward the target, never farther away.

The author uses a fixed target to prevent discretionary greed and a structural stop to scratch when the original trade premise fails.

## Research use

Specify order type, fill price, buffer, slippage, target, initial invalidation, and update timing. If stop and target are reachable within one bar, use event data or a declared conservative collision rule.
