# Chapter 14 — The Tipping Point Technique

## Core lesson

The tipping point is the price beyond which the trade's technical premise is no longer valid. The initial point is commonly one pip beyond the signal bar or setup high/low. If price surpasses it by the declared increment, scratch; otherwise remain in the trade.

As favorable structure develops, replace the old point with a newer one closer to the target. The target remains 10 pips in the original method, and the stop never moves away from it. Typical realized technical risk is about 6–7 pips despite the 10-pip maximum protection.

## Research use

Express updates as a causal state machine: which completed event creates a new level, when it becomes active, and how same-event collisions resolve. Otherwise trailing logic will leak future information.
