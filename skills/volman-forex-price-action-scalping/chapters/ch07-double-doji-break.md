# Chapter 7 — Double Doji Break (DD)

## Structure

A trend or new trend pulls back roughly 40–60%, commonly toward the 20 EMA area. Near the pullback end, at least two adjacent dojis or small candles show a temporary balance. The book accepts small bars—normally no more than about 3 pips—not only textbook zero-body dojis.

For a long, the signal bar is the member with the highest high; for a short, the lowest low. Its position inside the group does not matter. A later entry bar must break that trend-side extreme.

## Filters

Prefer an orderly pullback, intact trend pressure, and a clear 10-pip path. If trend-side extremes among the small bars are spread more than about one pip, assess trend strength carefully; a gap beyond roughly two pips in a weak trend is commonly a skip.

## Failure mode

Two small candles alone are not DD. Without trend, pullback, and path context they are only indecision.
