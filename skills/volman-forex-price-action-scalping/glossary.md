# Glossary

- **70-tick chart**: A chart whose bar completes after 70 transactions in the author's feed; it is provider-dependent and not a 70-second chart.
- **20 EMA**: Visual guide to trend and pullback location, never a mandatory support/resistance line.
- **Signal bar / signal line**: The bar and price extreme whose break would activate a setup.
- **Entry bar**: The later bar that actually crosses the frozen signal line.
- **Clear path**: Enough unobstructed price space from entry to target.
- **Pre-breakout tension**: Compression against a boundary showing limited ability to push price away.
- **Tipping point**: Technical invalidation level; crossing it by the specified increment requires a scratch.
- **DD**: Double Doji Break, a trend-pullback compression entry.
- **FB**: First Break, the first substantial pullback after a strong new surge.
- **SB**: Second Break, the second with-trend attack after the first lacks continuation.
- **BB**: Block Break, a directional break from a compact minirange.
- **RB**: Range Break, a prepared break of the original boundary of a mature range.
- **IRB**: Inside Range Break, a small block traded within a larger range, often as a boomerang.
- **ARB**: Advanced Range Break, an entry formed outside the range after the initial break.
- **False break**: A poorly prepared drive through a boundary that is prone to exhaustion and reversal.
- **Tease break**: A small premature poke through a boundary before sufficient buildup.
- **Round-number zone**: A psychologically visible price area; 00/50 were the original EUR/USD anchors.
